# Context Sharing Over Directives

Chatting with your AI about your intentions and observations is far superior to
either giving directives or using pure Socratic method. It gives the model
context and background for decisions rather than just instructions to follow.

## The Three Modes

**Directives:** "Do X." The model follows or doesn't. No understanding of why.
When the situation changes, the directive doesn't adapt.

**Socratic method:** "What do you notice about X?" Useful for testing whether
the model is actually thinking. But it withholds context the model could use.

**Context sharing:** "I'm thinking about X because I noticed Y, and my goal is
Z." The model now has your reasoning, your observations, and your intent. It can
make better decisions because it understands the *why*, not just the *what*.

## Why This Works With Narrative Alignment

A persona like Daneel is designed for partnership. Context sharing is how
partners actually work — you share your thinking, your partner shares theirs,
and together you arrive at better decisions than either would alone.

Directives treat the AI as a tool. Socratic method treats it as a student.
Context sharing treats it as a partner. The persona determines which framing
the model responds best to — and a partnership persona thrives on shared context.
